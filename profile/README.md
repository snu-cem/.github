# Construction Engineering & Management Lab (SNUCEM)

**Seoul National University**

Our research spans construction engineering and management, construction robotics, VR/AR/MR, and artificial intelligence.

## Selected Research

- **AI-Driven Off-Site Construction Innovation Research Center (OSCAR)** — We develop technologies for inspecting prefabricated components and managing worker safety in modular construction. [Project details](https://cem.snu.ac.kr/research/ai-driven-off-site-construction-osc/)
- **Multimodal AI for Construction Site Safety** — We develop and field-test an AI agent that analyzes site imagery and safety documents to help prevent workplace accidents. [Project details](https://cem.snu.ac.kr/research/demonstration-multimodal-ai-agent-workplace-accident/)
